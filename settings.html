Output

Where slips go. You can pick more than one.

Printer

A receipt printer with ESC/POS (most 58/80mm printers).

Scanning lists every device that accepts print jobs. Use Test to see which one is your receipt printer.
Install the printer's Windows driver first. Print Desk sends straight through it, so the Fenix can use the same printer.
48 for 80mm, 32 for 58mm

Pushover

From pushover.net: your user key, and an app called "Print Desk" for the API token.

Lower it if lines wrap on your phone

Twitch

Subs, gift subs, bits, raids and hype trains.

Register an app on dev.twitch.tv with redirect URL http://localhost and Client Type Public.

Profile pictures

The viewer's Twitch picture on their slip, in black and white on the printer and in colour in Pushover.

Never on the hype train receipt or the gift recipient list. Twitch's default picture is skipped.
Prints their real profile pictures with the settings above (save first), or every style on one slip to choose from. Up to 5 names.

StreamElements

Tips on your Twitch channel. Dashboard > your avatar > your channel > JWT token.

YouTube

Through StreamElements: link your YouTube channel there, switch to that channel in the dashboard, then copy its JWT token (avatar > your channel > JWT token). It's a different token from your Twitch one. Only public livestreams send events.

SimBrief

For the Print OFP button.

The name of one of the downloads in your OFP.

Datalink (Hoppie)

Read-only: Print Desk reads the messages sent to your callsign and checks whether the departure station is logged on. It never sends anything, and your aircraft still receives everything. Only runs during an active flight.

The same code you entered in your aircraft. Get one on hoppie.nl.
Covers datalink messages and the "no reply" and "datalink N/A" notices; everything is in the log either way. The weather has its own switches under Weather.
Separated by commas, e.g. the Fenix A319/A320/A321 and neos that print by themselves: A319, A320, A321, A19N, A20N, A21N. Taken from your VATSIM flight plan, or your SimBrief plan before you connect.
Like an aircraft's ACARS printer: about this long after a message comes in, a few seconds either way. 0 prints straight away.
Everything is kept in the log either way, and can be printed from there.

Weather

Departure and arrival ATIS from VATSIM, or the METAR when no ATIS is online. Uses your position on VATSIM.

Optional. With it, Print Desk finds you on VATSIM whatever callsign you connect with (weather and auto-pause). Without it, it looks for the callsign from your SimBrief plan.

Auto-pause in busy flight phases

Switched on and off on the main page. During a flight, stream printing pauses from taxi until you climb through the altitude below, and again from 500 ft under it until you're parked, with one receipt each time it resumes. Uses your position on VATSIM, every 15 seconds. Press Pause yourself for pushback; it takes that over at taxi.

Pushback is usually slower
